2024 Winner for International Card Manufacturers Association (International Card Manufacturers Association — ICMA) Élan award for Best ID Card

Here is a post on LinkedIn about one of the projects I contributed in (Wisconsin): LinkedIn Post WI

CBN is thrilled to announce that alongside our partners at the Wisconsin Department of Transportation | Wisconsin DMV, we have once again been honored with the prestigious International Card Manufacturers Association (International Card Manufacturers Association — ICMA) Élan award for Best ID Card. The 2024 award is for Wisconsin’s Real ID Over 21 Driver’s License and marks the fourth consecutive year that CBN has won the award. The ICMA Élan award recognizes the ‘best of the best’ in the global card manufacturing industry, making it the pinnacle of card excellence.

This award highlights the depth and strength of partnership that CBN is proud to share with the Wisconsin DMV since 2015, delivering industry leading card security, counterfeit resistance, and ease of authentication.
